# Creative Commons icons

The license-element icons Creative Commons publishes for marking works with
its licenses, from https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/icons/
(`cc`, `by`, `sa`, `nc`, `nd`, `zero`). See
https://creativecommons.org/mission/downloads/ and Creative Commons' policies
for the terms of their use.

They're kept here (rather than loaded from Creative Commons) so that pages
make no third-party requests for them. Used by `LicenseBadge` in
`web/src/components.tsx`.
